About Yash Khalkar

Yash Khalkar is a backend-focused full-stack software engineer based in Nashik, India. He enjoys turning product requirements into dependable software, from clear user interfaces and mobile experiences to APIs, data models, authentication, and production deployment. His current work includes backend engineering at Skillminds LLP on Pheraas, with ownership across REST APIs, core workflows, and practical system design.

Yash previously contributed as a founding frontend developer at Zenzop, building an admin panel, a landing page, onboarding flows, and supporting backend routes. He also completed an AI internship through Infosys Springboard focused on machine learning applications in healthcare. Outside work, he has led more than 12 technology community events through GDG On Campus at MET and co-organized Hacktoberfest Nashik '24.

His working toolkit includes TypeScript, JavaScript, React, Next.js, React Native, Node.js, Express.js, Hono, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, Prisma, AWS, Docker, Vercel, and Cloudflare. He is studying Computer Engineering at Savitribai Phule Pune University.
